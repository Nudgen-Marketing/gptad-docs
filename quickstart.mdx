## 1. Tell us what you want to achieve

Email [contact@gptad.io](mailto:contact@gptad.io) with your website, product or service, target markets, and business goal. Include your preferred timing and an indicative media budget if you have one.

At this stage, share business context rather than passwords, customer lists, or account access.

## 2. Discuss fit and scope

We discuss your audience, offer, landing pages, and how you will measure success. We also identify eligibility or access questions that could affect a ChatGPT Ads campaign.

The next step may be campaign planning, a readiness assessment, or a proposal for ongoing support. If launch is not yet feasible, any preparation work needs its own agreed scope.

## 3. Review your proposal and agreement

Check the deliverables, agency fees, separate advertising budget, responsibilities, approval rules, and commercial terms. Resolve open questions with our team before accepting.

Read [Scope and agreement](/working-together/agreement) for a practical review checklist.

## 4. Accept the agreement

Accept your service agreement through the process provided by GPTAd. Once acceptance is confirmed, our team coordinates onboarding and any contractual prerequisites.

<Note>
We begin onboarding only after agreement acceptance. Accepting an agreement does not by itself approve a campaign launch or a budget increase.
</Note>

## 5. Join onboarding

We confirm your contacts, collect the agreed brief and assets, arrange necessary access, and plan the kickoff. Use the [onboarding checklist](/working-together/onboarding) when our team invites you to begin.
