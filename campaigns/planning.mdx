## Choose a primary outcome

Start with the action that matters to your business, such as a qualified inquiry or purchase. Define what counts, where it is recorded, and who can validate its quality. Clicks can help diagnose traffic; they do not establish business value on their own.

## Build the plan around customer needs

Describe the problem your customer is solving, why your offer fits, and what they should do next. We use this to shape creative and the landing page within the options available to your account.

OpenAI explains that context hints can guide ad matching but do not guarantee delivery in particular conversations. Treat them as relevance guidance when available. See [Ads in ChatGPT: The Basics](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001207-ads-in-chatgpt-the-basics).

## Make the landing page ready

- Match the offer, price, and claims in the ad.
- Show a clear next action and make forms or checkout work on mobile.
- Explain important conditions before a visitor submits or pays.
- Confirm that your business details and applicable privacy information are available.
- Test destination links and the conversion journey before launch.

Our team flags issues; your agreement identifies who implements website changes.

## Agree a test plan

Record the hypothesis, variants, primary metric, budget limit, review point, and conditions for pausing or changing the test. For example: test whether an offer focused on installation support produces better qualified inquiries than a general product message.

Choose the evaluation period based on expected traffic and the sales cycle. Avoid declaring a winner from a few clicks or changing several variables at once without recording what changed.

## Complete the campaign brief

The brief should bring together the objective, offer, audience and markets, creative, destinations, measurement, dates, media limits, and approvers. Any unknowns should have an owner and a next step.
