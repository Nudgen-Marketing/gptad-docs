## What you approve

Before launch, review the campaign brief and the version of each asset intended for publication. Your approval should cover:

- Ad copy, images, claims, and offer conditions.
- Landing page destinations and the customer journey.
- Intended markets and available audience settings.
- Start and end dates, media budget, and spending limits.
- Measurement approach and any unresolved limitations.

We record approval through the channel agreed at kickoff. Silence is not approval. Changes to an approved offer, budget, or scope need the authorization required by your agreement.

## Launch readiness

| Check | Responsibility |
| --- | --- |
| Claims are accurate and assets can be used | You confirm; GPTAd reviews the supplied material |
| Final creative and media budget are authorized | Your named approver |
| Destination and conversion journey work | GPTAd and your website owner, according to scope |
| Measurement is checked and gaps are documented | GPTAd and your analytics contact, according to scope |
| Access and campaign configuration are ready | GPTAd, where included and access is available |
| Platform review and eligibility requirements are met | Determined by the advertising platform |

Client approval does not replace platform approval. GPTAd cannot guarantee acceptance or a particular launch date while platform review or access remains unresolved. Refer to [OpenAI’s advertising policies](https://openai.com/policies/ad-policies/) for current platform requirements.

## After launch

We confirm delivery status and review spend, destination behavior, and available measurement. Reports can lag behind activity, so an empty or incomplete report is not proof that no spend occurred.

We optimize within the authority and limits agreed with you. Material budget increases and work outside scope require approval.

## Request a pause or report a problem

Contact your GPTAd lead promptly with the campaign name, issue, and requested action. For an urgent issue, use a subject such as “Urgent: campaign pause request”. See [Support and handover](/support).

A request is not confirmation that delivery has stopped. We confirm the action and status; reporting and final charges may arrive later.
