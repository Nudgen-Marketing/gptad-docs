## ChatGPT Ads support

An engagement can cover the following work. Your proposal identifies which deliverables are included.

| Area | What the work can include | What you contribute |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Strategy and readiness | Business goals, audience needs, market and access checks, test plan | Offer, priorities, budget range, and business constraints |
| Creative | Ad concepts, copy, asset recommendations, and revisions | Brand assets, factual evidence, and approvals |
| Landing pages | Recommendations to align the page with the ad and improve the conversion journey | Website owner and approval for changes |
| Measurement | Measurement plan, tracking requirements, and validation where included | Analytics contact and access after onboarding begins |
| Campaign delivery | Setup and launch support where access and eligibility allow | Approval of the campaign and media budget |
| Optimization | Performance reviews and agreed tests | Lead quality or sales feedback and decisions |

## Is your business ready?

A useful starting point is a clear offer, a working website, an identifiable customer, and a meaningful action you want visitors to take. You also need someone who can approve the work and a budget you are prepared to test.

We assess launch feasibility for your advertiser and market. Availability of the advertising channel does not establish that your particular business, account, or campaign is eligible.

## Paid advertising and organic discovery

ChatGPT Ads is a paid channel. SEO and answer engine optimization (AEO) concern organic discovery. If you need those services, we can discuss them as a separate or explicitly included scope.

Buying ads does not buy influence over ChatGPT answers. OpenAI describes the separation between advertising and answers in its [advertising principles](https://openai.com/index/our-approach-to-advertising-and-expanding-access/).

## Set realistic expectations

We do not guarantee platform approval, placement in a particular conversation, lead volume, revenue, or return on ad spend. We agree the work and the way we will evaluate it before delivery begins.
