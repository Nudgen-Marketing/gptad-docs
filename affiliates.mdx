Earn **10% of GPTAd service fees** for as long as your referred client remains a paying customer. Advertising spend is excluded.

The program is for consultants, creators, agencies, and people who connect businesses with growth services. If you know a business looking for SEO, AI discovery, or ChatGPT advertising support, start with a conversation about whether GPTAd is a fit.

[Book a partner call](https://cal.com/toannhu/30min).

## How it works

1. **Talk with GPTAd.** Discuss your network, the clients we can help, and referral attribution and payout arrangements before making introductions.
2. **Introduce a business.** Connect a potential client with GPTAd. We discuss their needs and the services that make sense for them.
3. **Earn commission.** When your referred client pays for GPTAd services, you earn 10% of those service fees for as long as they remain a paying client.

A referral or partner call does not start client onboarding or authorize work or advertising spend. Referred clients follow the same [agreement-first process](/quickstart): service agreement acceptance, onboarding, campaign preparation, and separate launch approval.

## What the commission covers

| Payment | Commission treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Paid GPTAd service fees from your referred client | 10% commission |
| The client’s advertising spend | Excluded |

“Lifetime” means you continue to earn commission for as long as the referred client remains a paying GPTAd customer. Earnings depend on actual service-fee payments.

<Note>
For illustration only: $1,000 in paid GPTAd service fees produces $100 in commission. This is an example, not an earnings guarantee. Advertising spend is excluded.
</Note>

## Referral attribution and payouts

We discuss how referrals are attributed and how commissions are paid during partner onboarding, so you understand the arrangements before making introductions. This guide does not set a payout schedule, payment threshold, or attribution window.

The applicable agreement governs the detailed working arrangements. A partner conversation is separate from a referred client’s service agreement and onboarding.

## Common questions

### Does advertising spend count toward commission?

No. The revenue share applies only to GPTAd service fees paid by referred clients, not their advertising budget.

### Is the commission a one-time payment?

No. You earn 10% of service fees for as long as your referred client remains a paying GPTAd customer. Commission depends on their actual payments.
