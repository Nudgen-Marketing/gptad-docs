## How the relationship works

You bring your business knowledge, brand requirements, and approval authority. We turn the agreed brief into a campaign plan, prepare the work, and review results with you. Your agreement defines the deliverables, responsibilities, fees, and working arrangements.

Campaign launch depends on advertiser eligibility, account access, platform review, and your approval. Planning does not guarantee launch or results.
