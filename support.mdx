## Contact the team

Use the contact and communication channel agreed at kickoff. For general questions or if you cannot reach your usual contact, email [contact@gptad.io](mailto:contact@gptad.io).

Include your business name, the relevant campaign or deliverable, the issue, its impact, and the decision or action you need. Add a screenshot or link when useful, with sensitive information removed.

Response times and service availability follow your agreement. These docs do not promise round-the-clock monitoring.

## Urgent campaign issues

For unexpected spend, an incorrect offer, a broken landing page, or an access concern, state the issue clearly and whether you are requesting a pause. Use an “Urgent” subject line and the escalation channel agreed with your team.

Wait for confirmation of the action taken. Do not assume that sending a message stops delivery immediately.

## Changes, pauses, and cancellation

Send requests to your GPTAd contact. We review the effect on active work, delivery, media spend, and timing. Your agreement governs notice, billing, refunds if applicable, and termination. A campaign pause and the end of an agency engagement are different requests; specify which you need.

## Handover checklist

At the end of an engagement, coordinate the following according to your agreement:

- Confirm which campaigns will stop and who manages any remaining activity.
- Reconcile available spend and outstanding invoices.
- Transfer the agreed deliverables, reports, and relevant working context.
- Confirm account ownership and remove permissions that are no longer needed.
- Agree how retained information and any deletion requests are handled under the applicable terms.

## Common questions

### Do I need to sign up before speaking with GPTAd?

No. Start with a conversation about your business. Onboarding begins only after you accept the service agreement.

### Does accepting an agreement mean my ads are live?

No. We still need the campaign inputs, necessary access, your launch approval, and any required platform approval.

### Is advertising spend included in the agency fee?

Do not assume so. Agency fees and media spend are agreed separately. Your proposal and agreement specify the payment arrangements.

### Can you guarantee leads or recommendations in ChatGPT?

No. Outcomes depend on the offer, market, campaign, measurement, and platform delivery. Paid advertising does not buy organic recommendations.
