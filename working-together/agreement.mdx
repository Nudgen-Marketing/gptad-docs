Your proposal and service agreement define the engagement. These guides explain the workflow; they do not replace your agreement or add commercial commitments.

## Review before accepting

- **Scope:** Services, deliverables, exclusions, revision rounds, and dependencies.
- **Timing:** Milestones, client input deadlines, and what happens if access or approvals are delayed.
- **Fees:** Agency fees, invoice schedule, applicable taxes, and payment terms.
- **Media budget:** Advertising spend separately from agency fees, the payer, spending limits, and who can authorize changes.
- **Ownership and access:** Account ownership, asset usage rights, permissions, and handover responsibilities.
- **Approvals:** Named decision makers, approval channel, and which routine optimizations can proceed within agreed limits.
- **Reporting:** Metrics, reporting frequency, meetings, and data sources.
- **Changes and ending the engagement:** Change requests, renewal, notice periods, cancellation, and any applicable refund terms.

Ask our team to clarify anything missing before you accept. Prices, minimum budgets, response times, and cancellation terms are specific to your agreement.

## Keep fees and advertising spend separate

Agency fees pay for the agreed services. Media spend pays for ad delivery. Confirm who pays the advertising platform and how spend is reconciled. Do not assume that a service fee includes media spend.

## Change the scope deliberately

Send your requested change to your GPTAd contact. We clarify the effect on fees, timing, and deliverables and obtain the required approval before additional work proceeds. Budget increases require approval from your authorized contact.

## After acceptance

Once acceptance is confirmed, we arrange [onboarding](/working-together/onboarding). The campaign still needs a completed plan, required access, platform review where applicable, and [launch approval](/campaigns/approvals).
