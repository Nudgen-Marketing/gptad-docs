<Note>
This checklist is for clients whose service agreement has been accepted. If you are still exploring, start with [Become a client](/quickstart). Wait for our team to coordinate access requests.
</Note>

## Confirm your working team

Name a day-to-day contact, a person authorized to approve spend and creative, and the people responsible for your website and analytics. One person can cover multiple roles.

At kickoff, we agree how to communicate, where approvals are recorded, the reporting schedule, and the first milestones.

## Prepare your campaign brief

| Information | What to share |
| --- | --- |
| Business and offer | Website, products or services, pricing, differentiators, and offer limitations |
| Goal | The business outcome and primary conversion action |
| Customers | Intended audience, markets, languages, and exclusions |
| Budget and timing | Approved media limit, proposed dates, seasonality, and dependencies |
| Brand | Logos, images, tone of voice, usage rights, and required wording |
| Evidence | Support for product claims, offers, testimonials, and certifications |
| Website | Proposed landing pages and the contact responsible for updates |
| Measurement | Existing analytics, conversion definitions, and any known data gaps |

Share only the information needed for the agreed work. Do not include personal customer data in the initial brief.

## Arrange access securely

We identify the accounts and permissions required for your scope. Use individual invitations and the minimum permissions needed, with multifactor authentication where available. Keep ownership and recovery access with your authorized business representatives.

Never send passwords, recovery codes, payment card details, or API secrets by email. Agree a secure transfer method if sensitive information is necessary.

## Confirm readiness at kickoff

Before moving to production work, confirm the accepted agreement, named approvers, agreed brief, available assets, required access, measurement owner, and open dependencies. If something is missing, we record the owner and next step and adjust the schedule together.
